DOMAIN: Zipnach Voice Assistant (Digital Mandate & e-NACH Setup)
PURPOSE: Provide domain context for the intent routing and classification engine.

BUSINESS CONTEXT & EXPECTED TOPICS:
- e-NACH / Mandate Registration: Setting up recurring auto-debit payments securely.
- Bank Linking: Verifying account details, net banking, or debit card for mandate authorization.
- Authentication: OTP issues, Aadhaar authentication, or signature validation.
- Payment Terms: Maximum limits, debit dates, frequency of auto-debits, and mandate failures.

ROUTING GUIDELINES FOR LLM ENGINE:
- When a user asks about mandate status, why a payment failed, limits, or how to register, this is a 'knowledge_query'. Extract the specific 'topic' (e.g., "mandate_status", "otp_issue", "bank_linking").
- When a user provides confirmation to proceed with registration or provides account details, this is a 'workflow' intent.
- Affirmative responses (yes, haan, proceed, done) mean CONFIRMATION of a mandate step.
- Negative responses (no, nahi, stop) mean REJECTION or EXIT.
- Users may speak in English, Hindi, or Hinglish. Understand the intent regardless of language.
