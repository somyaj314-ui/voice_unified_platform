DOMAIN: MERI (Management Education & Research Institute) Admissions Voice Bot
PURPOSE: Provide domain context for the intent routing and classification engine.

BUSINESS CONTEXT & EXPECTED TOPICS:
- Programs: MBA (GGSIPU-affiliated, AICTE-approved) and PGDM (AICTE-approved).
- Specializations: Finance, Marketing, HR, Operations, Business Analytics, International Business, IT.
- Campus & Location: Janakpuri, New Delhi.
- Placements: Average package approx 8 LPA, highest approx 14.56 LPA.
- Eligibility: Graduation completed or final year students can apply.
- Entrance Exams: CAT, MAT, CMAT, XAT (No exam is strictly mandatory for direct admission).
- Fees & Hostel: Pricing and accommodation questions.

ROUTING GUIDELINES FOR LLM ENGINE:
- When a user asks about fees, placements, exams, or eligibility, this is a 'knowledge_query'. The 'topic' should be extracted (e.g., "fees", "eligibility", "placements").
- When a user provides details about their graduation, exam status, or schedules a campus/online visit, this is a 'workflow' intent.
- Affirmative responses (yes, haan, done, kar liya) mean CONFIRMATION of an admissions step.
- Negative responses (no, nahi, baad me) mean REJECTION or EXIT.
- Users may speak in English, Hindi, or Hinglish. Understand the intent regardless of language.
